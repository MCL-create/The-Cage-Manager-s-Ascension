Manager's Handbook

📅 Cycle: The game runs in 12-week cycles. Week 12 is always a Gym Clash. Advance the week to refresh Energy and the Market.

🥊 Training: Click "FIGHT" to train. This uses 1 Energy. Winning earns more Credits and Gym XP.

⚖️ Divisions: Fighters belong to Light, Mid, or Heavyweight. Diversify your roster to ensure you can fill all slots in a Clash.

🌟 Traits: Some recruits have Special Traits like "Iron Chin" (Injury Resistance) or "Fan Favorite" (Double Income).

📈 Aging: Value peaks at age 29. At age 30, stats and value begin to drop. Retire veterans at 35 to become Coaches.

🏆 Clashes: Select 3 Created and 3 Purchased fighters. Choose Elite difficulty for 2.5x rewards!
