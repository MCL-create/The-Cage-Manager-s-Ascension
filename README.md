🥋 The Cage: Manager Ascension
The Cage: Manager Ascension is a lightweight, browser-based MMA management simulator. Take the role of a gym owner, recruit rising talent, manage their training, and climb the ranks of the professional fighting world.

🚀 How to Play
Access: Simply open the index.html file in any modern web browser.

GitHub Pages: To play on your phone, go to your GitHub repository Settings > Pages and set the branch to "main". This will give you a live website link to share with the boys!

🎮 Core Gameplay Loop
1. Build Your Brand
Personalize: Tap the Gym Logo on the Home screen to upload your own team photo.

Rename: Click on "The Cage Gym" text to type in your own custom Gym Name.

2. Recruitment & Workshop
Workshop: Create a custom fighter for 💰500. Use the slider to determine if they are a pure Grappler, a Striker, or a Balanced martial artist.

Market: Visit the Market to scout pre-generated talent with unique backgrounds.

3. Training & Management
Stats: Fighters have two main stats: Striking and Grappling.

Energy: Each manager starts the week with 10⚡ Energy.

Training: Spend 💰100 to improve stats. Higher stats significantly increase the chance of winning fights.

4. Entering The Cage
Standard Match (⚡ 2): Fight random opponents. Difficulty increases every week.

Call Out (⚡ 4): Challenge specific legendary Rivals (like Iron Malik or The Ghost) for massive Point and XP rewards.

5. Gym Ascension
Gym Level: Winning fights earns XP. Leveling up unlocks Sponsorships (weekly point bonuses).

Hall of Fame: Once a fighter reaches 5 wins, they can be "Ascended" to the Hall of Fame to give your gym a massive XP boost.

💾 Saving & Persistence
The game uses Local Storage. Progress is automatically saved to the browser on that specific device.

Note: If you play on a different device or clear your browser cache, you will start a new career.
